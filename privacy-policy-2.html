Introduction

Sites by Sara ("we," "us," or "our") operates sitesbysara.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our Site or use our services. By using our Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number
  • Account data: username, password (encrypted), preferences
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the site
  • Device data: device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Payment data: processed securely via QuickBooks Online — we do not store this

How we collect data

We collect data through: account registration forms, contact and inquiry forms, cookies and tracking technologies, analytics tools such as Google Analytics and Brevo, our newsletter sign-up.

Why we use your data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our site and services
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send transactional emails
  • To send marketing communications, if you have opted in
  • To analyze site usage and improve our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These include: essential cookies (required for the Site to function), analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics), and marketing cookies (e.g. Meta Pixel and Google Ads). You can manage or withdraw your consent to non-essential cookies at any time via our cookie settings banner or your browser settings.

SMS / Text Messaging

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from Sites by Sara, we collect your mobile phone number and a record of consent (timestamp, IP address, and the page where consent was given).

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors that support our services (e.g., customer service) is permitted. All other categories of use exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies; on average 1–2 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for assistance. For questions, contact team@sitesbysara.com.
