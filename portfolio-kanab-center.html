Web Design Medium WordPress Site (31 Pages) Events & Venues

The Kanab Center is a 15,000+ square foot events and convention facility in Kanab, Southern Utah, hosting conventions, corporate meetings, weddings, performances, and the town's annual rodeo. Its location amid Southern Utah's red rock landscape and proximity to national parks is central to the pitch — the center encourages event attendees to "stay a little longer" and explore.

A venue this multi-purpose needed a site that could serve very different visitors at once: an event planner comparing convention spaces, a couple researching wedding venues, and a local checking gym reservations or the rodeo schedule — all without the site feeling scattered.

We built the Kanab Center across 31 pages covering Venue & Amenities, Upcoming Events, Equipment Rentals, Gym Reservations, a media gallery, and dedicated Rodeo event pages, giving each use case its own clear path.
