Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our newsletter management and email marketing services.

What's included in Newsletter Management? +
Ongoing template management and campaign sends at your chosen frequency: Monthly (1 send/month) at $150/mo, Bi-Weekly at $275/mo, or Weekly at $450/mo.
Do I need Template Design before Newsletter Management starts? +
Only if you don't already have a branded newsletter template. Template Design ($500 one-time) covers a branded template and your first campaign layout. If you already have a template, you can start directly with Newsletter Management.
What if I need more sends than my monthly plan covers? +
Subsequent Campaign Send ($250 one-time, per send) covers layout, testing, and sending an extra campaign beyond what your plan includes.
Can you import our existing email list? +
Yes. Email List Import ($65 one-time) is a done-for-you import of your existing contacts.
What is Deliverability & Domain Verification? +
A one-time service ($180) that sets up mail deliverability and verifies your sending domain, so your newsletters are less likely to land in spam.
Can I see how my newsletter is performing? +
Yes. Email Metrics Reporting ($35 one-time, per report) covers open and click performance for a campaign.
