Why It Matters

Outdated WordPress sites are exactly what hackers look for.

As PHP upgrades roll out across web servers, an unmaintained WordPress site becomes old and vulnerable — and hackers specifically target sites running outdated WordPress and PHP versions. Suddenly upgrading everything after years of neglect is risky too, since an outdated backup is often no safer to roll back to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our WordPress hosting & maintenance plans.

What's included in a WHAM plan? +
Domain name registration and renewal, a secure SSL license, WordPress maintenance, email account setup, 24/7 technical support, professional WordPress hosting, and a 99.999% uptime guarantee.
How often are backups taken? +
Daily. Your website files and database are backed up daily and saved in two separate locations.
What happens if my WordPress site gets hacked? +
Your site is immediately reinstated to its most recent clean backup copy.
How often are plugins, themes, and WordPress core updated? +
Plugin updates and security patches are applied weekly, theme updates monthly (child themes weekly when an update is available), and WordPress core updates are applied as soon as WordPress posts them.
Can I pay for a WHAM plan annually? +
Yes. Squeeze Page is $855/yr and Standard is $1,710/yr, each a 5% discount versus paying monthly. Premium ($3,600/yr) and Deluxe ($3,750/yr) are billed annually.
What if I need extra development hours beyond my maintenance plan? +
WHAM covers hosting, security, and maintenance — not general development work. For extra hours, see our Hourly Dev Blocks page for hourly rates and a monthly hour-block retainer.
