The Car Analogy

Why WordPress needs WHAM

WordPress websites require maintenance. Running one without it is like driving your car with no oil changes, no routine service, and no insurance.

Without WHAM:

Your site breaks down sooner.

WordPress, its themes, and its plugins need regular updates to keep working together.

You won't know when something goes wrong.

If your site gets hacked or stops working properly, you often won't find out until it's too late — and by then the damage is done.

Your liability is much higher.

Like driving uninsured, there's no safety net when something happens.

Think of WHAM as maintenance, registration, and insurance for your website. It keeps a powerful vehicle running safely.

The Alternative

The alternative: a simple HTML site

WordPress is a content management system (CMS) — software that lets you edit your own site, but that also has to be constantly updated and watched. If you're not making a lot of changes to your website yourself, consider converting it from WordPress to a non-CMS site coded in HTML, JavaScript, and PHP. It has far fewer maintenance requirements and costs.

A coded HTML site doesn't need the ongoing maintenance — only hosting. That's why HTML hosting is $25/month instead of $150/month.
