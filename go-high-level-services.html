Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Go High Level setup and management.

What is Go High Level (GHL)? +
Go High Level is an all-in-one CRM, funnel, and marketing automation platform. It combines your pipeline, funnels, calendar, and follow-up automations into one workspace instead of several disconnected tools.
How much does GHL setup and management cost? +
A one-time setup fee, plus ongoing monthly management, with a minimum 6-month engagement. See the pricing above for current rates.
What's included in GHL setup? +
Sub-account setup, CRM pipelines mapped to your sales process, funnel/landing pages, automations, and calendar and booking integration.
Why is there a minimum 6-month engagement? +
Setup is a one-time investment, and pipelines and automations need time to run and be tuned against real leads before results are clear — the 6-month minimum keeps management in place long enough for that tuning to pay off.
Can GHL replace my other marketing tools? +
For most small businesses, yes — GHL can replace a separate CRM, funnel builder, appointment scheduler, and follow-up/SMS tool with one connected workspace.
Can you migrate my existing CRM into GHL? +
Yes. Existing contacts, pipeline stages, and automations from another CRM can be rebuilt inside GHL as part of setup.
What if I want a standalone CRM instead of the full GHL platform? +
That's a separate service — see our CRM Build, Setup & Management page for standalone CRM builds, without the funnel and marketing automation tools bundled into GHL.
