Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about GoHighLevel setup and management.

What is GoHighLevel? +
GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one platform for your CRM, funnels, calendar, email, texting, and follow-up automation, in one workspace instead of several separate tools.
Who owns the account? +
You do. We set it up on your own GoHighLevel billing, under your name, from day one. We manage it on your behalf for as long as you'd like.
What will GoHighLevel cost me each month? +
GoHighLevel's plans start at $97/month, plus usage for texts and calls. You pay GoHighLevel directly. Our build and management fees are separate. We're a GoHighLevel affiliate, so we may earn a referral commission when you sign up through our link, at no extra cost to you.
Why is Management billed in 6-month blocks? +
Automations need time to run against real leads before you can see what's working. Six months gives us room to roll out features, measure results, and tune them. Ending a block early carries an early cancellation fee, as outlined in our Master Service Agreement.
What counts as a feature rollout? +
One new capability per month, such as review collection, social scheduling, or appointment reminders, fully tested with team training. Larger features like landing pages use two months of rollouts, and extra-large features like an AI Receptionist use three, or can be quoted as a project.
Can you move my existing CRM into GoHighLevel? +
Yes. We can bring over your existing contacts and pipeline stages, and rebuild your automations inside GHL as part of setup.
Can GoHighLevel replace my other tools? +
For most small businesses, yes. It can replace a separate CRM, funnel builder, appointment scheduler, and email and texting tools with one connected workspace.
