Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Walmart ads management.

What's included in Walmart Ads Management? +
Every tier includes first month campaign setup & management, ongoing monthly optimization, and monthly reporting. A $350 one-time onboarding fee applies.
What are the different Walmart Ads Management tiers? +
Four tiers: Starter at $350/mo, Growth at $550/mo, Bloom at $850/mo, and Harvest at $1,000/mo.
Does this include creating my Walmart product listings or store page? +
No — Walmart Ads Management covers ad campaign setup and ongoing management for your existing Walmart listings. Your first month covers campaign setup, followed by ongoing monthly optimization and reporting every month after.
Is there a setup fee? +
Yes, a $350 one-time onboarding fee applies in addition to your monthly tier.
Do you offer advertising services for Amazon as well? +
Yes, we also work with Amazon sellers. See our Amazon Listing & Store Setup page for Amazon-specific listing and store services.
How is Walmart Ads Management priced? +
A flat monthly rate based on your tier ($350 to $1,000/mo), plus a one-time $350 onboarding fee.
