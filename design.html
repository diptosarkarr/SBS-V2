Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about graphic design and branding.

Do you design logos and full brand identities? +
Yes. We design everything from a simple logo to a complete brand identity, including colors, fonts, and a style guide.
What is a brand style guide? +
A brand style guide documents your logo, colors, fonts, and styling so everything you create looks consistent.
Do you design print materials, not just digital branding? +
Yes. We design business cards, envelopes, posters, postcards, and mailers, and can handle printing for mailers.
Can you update an existing design? +
Yes. For example, we can update an existing business card design file for you.
Can you help me rebrand an existing business? +
Yes. If your brand is ready to evolve, we can help with rebranding, from research through to a full style guide.
