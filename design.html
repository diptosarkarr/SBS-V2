Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our graphic design and branding services.

What's the difference between Basic Logo and Logo Design Package? +
Basic Logo ($500) delivers PNG/JPG files only, no source files, with up to 5 revisions. Logo Design Package ($1,000) includes unlimited revisions, source files, and a business card design.
What's included in the Branding Package? +
Logo plus source files, defined colors and fonts, styling conventions, and taglines/hashtags — for $2,200 one-time.
What's the difference between Branding Package and Brand Development? +
Branding Package ($2,200) builds your brand identity from scratch. Brand Development ($8,500) is a deeper engagement for existing brands — focus groups/surveys, rebranding analysis, and a full style guide.
Do you design print materials, not just digital branding? +
Yes. Business card design, envelope design, poster design, postcard design, and mailer/EDDM design and printing are all available individually.
Can you update an existing business card design? +
Yes. Business Card Edit ($15 per card) updates an existing business card design file.
How is Mailer/EDDM Design & Printing priced? +
Custom quote. It includes custom mailer design, EDDM presort and postage coordination, and trucking to the sortation facility, so pricing depends on your mailer's size and distribution.
