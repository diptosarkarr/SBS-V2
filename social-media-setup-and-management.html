Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our social media setup and management.

What's included in Social Media Management? +
Ongoing content posting across up to 3 networks. Three tiers by posting volume: 5 posts/month, 12 posts/month, or 20 posts/month. See the pricing above for current rates.
Do I need Social Account Setup before Social Media Management starts? +
Only if you don't already have social profiles. Social Account Setup (a one-time fee per profile) handles handle reservation and a branded profile image for each new profile. If your accounts already exist, you can start directly with Social Media Management.
How many social networks are covered? +
Up to 3 networks are included in every Social Media Management tier.
What's the difference between the three Social Media Management tiers? +
Posting volume: 5 posts/month, 12 posts/month, or 20 posts/month — all three cover up to 3 networks. See the pricing above for current rates.
Is Social Account Setup priced per platform or one flat fee? +
Per profile. Social Account Setup is a one-time fee for each individual social profile set up.
Do you run paid social ads too, not just organic posting? +
Yes — paid social advertising (Meta Ads and Meta Boost Posts) is a separate service. See our Google & Meta Ads page for ad campaign setup and management.
