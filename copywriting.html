Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our copywriting and content writing services.

How is Content Writing priced? +
By word count, per piece: 150, 250, 500, 750, 1000, or 1500 words. See the pricing above for current rates.
What's included with Content Writing? +
Standalone written content, priced per word count you choose.
Can you add a blog post to my existing blog? +
Yes. Blog Post Add-On (Single) is a one-time fee for one additional blog post, roughly 1,000-1,500 words, SEO optimized, with an image sourced and uploaded for you.
What's the difference between Human Content Writing and AI-Human Hybrid Content Writing? +
Human Content Writing is fully human-written, priced per word. AI-Human Hybrid Content Writing blends AI drafting with human editing, priced per word, with a minimum word count. See the pricing above for current per-word rates.
Is there a minimum word count? +
Content Writing starts at 150 words. AI-Human Hybrid Content Writing has a minimum word count. Human Content Writing has no stated minimum.
Do you also design the pages this content goes on? +
Yes — content writing pairs naturally with our web design and graphic design services. See our Graphic Design & Branding page or our website services if you need both.
