Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our WooCommerce store builds and design refresh service.

What's the difference between the four WooCommerce store packages? +
The four packages are scoped by how many products you're launching with: a single-page Squeeze Page store with 3 products ($3,500), a Starter store with 15 products ($7,000), a Standard store with 50 products ($7,500), or a Premium store with 100 products ($8,000).
What's included with every WooCommerce store build? +
Every package includes full SEO metadata, a mobile-responsive design, and hands-on training so you know how to manage your products after launch.
I already have a WooCommerce store — do I need a full rebuild to update its look? +
No. Our WooCommerce Design Refresh updates the theme and overall presentation of an existing WooCommerce store for a flat $1,000, without a full rebuild.
What does the WooCommerce Design Refresh actually update? +
It refreshes the theme and overall presentation across your Shop, Products, Cart, and Checkout pages. It requires that you already have an existing WooCommerce site in place.
Can I start with a smaller product catalog and grow it later? +
Yes. Many clients start with the package that matches their current catalog size and grow into a larger package as their product line expands.
Does the store price include payment processing setup? +
Store pricing covers the design, development, and product catalog setup. Payment processing / merchant account setup is available as a separate add-on — just let us know during your consultation.
