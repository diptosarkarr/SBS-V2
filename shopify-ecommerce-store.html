Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Shopify store builds, design refresh, and add-on services.

What's the difference between the four Shopify store packages? +
The four packages are scoped by how many products you're launching with: a single-page Squeeze Page store with 3 products ($2,500), a Starter store with 15 products ($4,000), a Standard store with 50 products ($4,500), or a Premium store with 100 products ($5,500).
What's included with every Shopify store build? +
Every package includes full SEO metadata, a mobile-responsive design, and hands-on training so you know how to manage your products after launch.
I already have a Shopify store — do I need a full rebuild to update its look? +
No. Our Shopify Design Refresh updates the theme and overall presentation of an existing Shopify store for a flat $1,500, without a full rebuild.
I just need a few more pages or products added — do I need the full Design Refresh? +
No. Add Pages and Add Products are separate add-ons for exactly that — improving the layout and presentation of new pages (sold in blocks of 5, $2,000) or new products (sold in blocks of 10, $300) without refreshing your entire store.
Can I start with a smaller product catalog and grow it later? +
Yes. Many clients start with the package that matches their current catalog size and grow into a larger package, or add more products with the Add Products add-on, as their product line expands.
Does the price include Shopify's subscription fee or payment processing? +
No. Pricing above covers Sites by Sara's design and development fees only. Shopify's monthly subscription and payment processing are billed directly by Shopify, separate from our fee.
