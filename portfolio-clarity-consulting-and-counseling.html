Clarity Consulting and Counseling is a concierge mental health therapy practice in Lehi, Utah, led by Claire Groll, LCSW. Rather than the one-size-fits-all model most therapy practices default to, Claire built her practice around flexible, personalized care — weekly sessions, expanded and intensive sessions, on-call support between appointments, and case management for individuals, families, and corporate clients alike.

A practice this personal needed a website that felt the same way: warm and direct, not clinical. We built Clarity Consulting and Counseling as a squeeze page — a single, tightly structured page that reads like a full site through strong section flow, without asking a potential client to dig through menus while they're already in a vulnerable moment.

The site leads with what Clarity offers and who it's for, introduces Claire and her credentials, and gets visitors to a call or contact form as quickly as possible — exactly what a concierge therapy practice needs from its front door.
