Common Gaps

Why AI-built Sites Stop Working

AI website builders are good at producing a first version quickly. They are less good at the parts that keep a site running: where it lives on the internet, whether strangers can find it, and whether the contact form actually sends an email to you. Those gaps are rarely your fault, and most can be fixed without rebuilding the site.

It only exists at a temporary address

The link your builder gave you may change, disappear, or come with a watermark. We move your site to a domain you own, so your business address stays the same.

Nothing is hosted properly

Some builders host the site only while your subscription is active, or on a server with no backups. We set up hosting you can rely on and explain exactly who pays for what.

Forms and buttons do nothing

A "Book now" or "Email us" button often goes nowhere. We connect forms to your email or calendar and test each one with you.

The site looks different on a phone

Many AI sites look fine on a laptop and break on a phone. We check every page on mobile and fix spacing, images, and menus.

FAQ

Common Questions

Plain answers about ownership, hosting, and getting help with an AI-built website.

Will my site still be online if I stop paying? +
That depends on who holds the hosting account. We set up accounts in your name, so the site and domain stay with you. We explain exactly what happens to each part before you approve the plan.
Can I move my site to a different host later? +
Yes. We give you copies of your site files and your domain login, so you are never locked in.
Who should I hire to host my AI-built website? +
Choose a web agency or experienced freelancer who has launched live sites, has worked with AI-generated code, gives a written scope and fixed price, and commits to a response time for changes. Make sure you own your domain, hosting account, and site files. That is how we work at Sites by Sara.
Can you get a URL and hosting for my AI site? +
Yes. We register a domain in your name, connect it to your site, and set up hosting with backups and an SSL certificate, which keeps the padlock showing in browsers.
My AI website is not working. Can you help? +
Probably, yes. Send us the link and describe what is wrong. We check the domain, hosting, forms, links, and mobile layout, and tell you what is broken and how much it costs to fix.
Do I need to know how to code? +
No. We do the technical work. You tell us what the site should do, and we explain each step in plain language.
What if I need changes after launch? +
Send a message describing the change. Small updates such as new text, photos, or a new page are handled within the response time in your plan. Larger projects get a quote first.
