Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Google and Meta ads management.

Do you manage both Google Ads and Meta Ads? +
Yes. We manage Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), together or separately.
Do you handle both setup and ongoing management? +
Yes. We build your campaigns and then manage and optimize them on an ongoing basis.
What's the difference between Meta Ads and boosting posts? +
Meta Ads is a full campaign built around your goals and audience. Boosting posts is a lighter option that promotes content you've already published.
Do I need ad accounts already set up? +
Not necessarily. If you already have Google Ads or Meta accounts, we'll review them. If not, tell us in your free consultation and we'll guide you.
Do you also manage Amazon or Walmart ads? +
Yes, as separate services. See our Amazon Store Setup & Ads Management and Walmart Ads Management pages.
