Security & Trust

How We Protect Your Business

When you trust us with your website, you're trusting us with your brand, your customers' data, and the logins that keep your business running. We take that seriously.

Before anyone on our team works on a client project, they review and sign a set of agreements that hold them to our standards for security and professionalism:

  • Non-Disclosure Agreement Your business information, plans, and data stay private.
  • Confidentiality & Intellectual Property Agreement Your information stays confidential, and the work we create for you belongs to you.
  • Data Security Addendum Clear rules for how we handle passwords, site access, and sensitive data.
  • Remote Work Addendum Secure practices apply wherever our team works, including device and network standards.
  • Code of Conduct Addendum Professional, respectful standards for how we work with you and your customers.
  • Team Member Agreement Defined responsibilities and accountability for everyone who touches your project.

It's one more way we treat your business like our own.
