Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about custom WordPress website design.

Why choose a custom WordPress website over a template or DIY builder? +
A custom site is designed around your brand and goals instead of a one-size-fits-all template. It tends to be faster, easier to grow, and more distinctive.
How long does it take to build a custom WordPress website? +
It depends on the size and complexity of your site and how quickly your content is ready. We set a clear timeline together during discovery.
Can I update the content on my website myself? +
Yes. Your site is built on WordPress, so you can edit text, images, and blog posts yourself without any coding. We are happy to show you how.
Will my website work well on mobile? +
Yes. Every site we build is designed to look and work great on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
Can I add more pages later? +
Yes. Your site can grow as your business does. Tell us what you have in mind and we will recommend the right approach.
