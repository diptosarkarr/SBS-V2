Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Site Audit & Priority Fixes service.

What's included in the Site Audit & Priority Fixes service? +
This service is $950 one-time, and includes a full-site performance analysis with up to 10 priority fixes identified.
What counts as a "priority fix"? +
Priority fixes are the top issues identified during your full-site performance analysis, ranked by impact. Up to 10 are included in your report.
Do you also fix the issues you find, or just identify them? +
This service identifies up to 10 priority fixes through a full-site performance analysis. If you'd like us to implement the fixes, that can be scoped separately based on what's found.
Can I add Google Analytics or Search Console setup with my audit? +
Yes. Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GA/GTM) Setup is $125 one-time, and Google Search Console (GSC) Setup is available for a custom quote.
What's the difference between GA/GTM Setup and GSC Setup? +
Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GA/GTM) Setup ($125 one-time) sets up analytics and tag manager tracking on your site. Google Search Console (GSC) Setup handles Search Console setup and verification, and is priced with a custom quote.
Is the Site Audit a one-time service or ongoing? +
It's a one-time engagement at $950, producing a full-site performance analysis and a prioritized list of up to 10 fixes.
