At Sites by Sara, we are committed to supporting various nonprofits and social giving initiatives.

Where We're Giving Back Now

2026 Campaigns

Salt Lake City School Board – Monthly Contributor

Strong schools build strong communities. Sites by Sara gives every month to support the Salt Lake City School Board and its work for students, teachers, and families across our city.

Table Sponsor – House of Prayer for All Peoples

Sites by Sara was proud to be a table sponsor and donor for House of Prayer for All Peoples. This continues our partnership with them, which began through our work with Life Is Beautiful Prayers.

SLC Police Kids Back to School Supplies Program

Every child deserves to start the school year ready to learn. We donated to the Salt Lake City Police Department's Back to School Supplies Program, which helps local kids head back to class with the supplies they need.

Mountain Town Music – Stage Sponsor

This is our third year in a row as a stage sponsor for Mountain Town Music. We're happy to help bring free live music to our mountain communities all summer long.

Jewish Family Service of Utah – Donation & Volunteer Time

We're still proud supporters of Jewish Family Service of Utah. This year our team gave both a financial donation and our own volunteer time to help JFSU serve individuals and families in need.

KRCL Community Radio – Monthly Contributor

Sites by Sara gives every month to KRCL 90.9 FM, Salt Lake's listener-supported community radio station. KRCL amplifies local voices, music, and stories.

Mountain West Chamber – Sponsor

We sponsor the Mountain West Chamber to support local businesses and the connections that help our regional economy grow.

Here's How We've Been Making An Impact

2025 Campaigns

Food Pantry Volunteering – Holiday Season

During the holiday season, the Sites by Sara team volunteered at the JFSU Food Pantry as part of our ongoing social giving efforts. It was a meaningful day of service spent supporting our local community together.

Community Outreach in Balanga City, Bataan, Philippines

Sites by Sara, Exquisite Network Inc., and MedServ Pharmacy partnered to support the elderlies of Bahay Puso and the Bahay Puso Apostolate Foundation. The team delivered food, medicine, essential supplies, and shared moments of joy, care, and connection.

Diwali Support – India Team

To celebrate Diwali, the Sites by Sara team in India provided essential supplies to a local orphanage. We are grateful for the opportunity to help spread joy, light, and kindness during this festive season.

Sponsored Gerald's Kindergarten Education

Public education is not free in Tanzania. Sites by Sara sponsored Gerald's kindergarten education through the Life Is Beautiful Prayers initiative.

Table Sponsor at Neighbors 4 Neighbors Casino Night Fundraiser

Sites by Sara proudly sponsored a blackjack table and contributed $1,500 in raffle prizes for the Casino Night fundraiser put on by Keller Williams Real Estate for SLC Neighbors 4 More Neighbors!
