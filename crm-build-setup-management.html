Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about CRM setup and management.

What is a CRM? +
A CRM keeps your contacts, conversations, and deals in one organized place, so nothing slips through the cracks and your team knows who to follow up with next.
Which CRM platforms can you work with? +
We work with popular platforms, including the ones listed above, and help you choose the one that suits your workflow, your team, and the tools you already use. Tell us your preference in your free consultation.
What can a CRM automate for my business? +
Common examples are follow-up messages sent automatically to new leads, website forms that feed straight into your CRM as new contacts, and lead scoring that shows you who is most ready to buy.
What is the difference between CRM setup and CRM management? +
Setup gets your CRM built and configured. Management keeps it accurate and working as your business changes. Many clients start with a build and add management later.
Can you work with a CRM I already have? +
Possibly. Tell us which CRM you use and how it's set up in your free consultation, and we'll let you know the right way to help.
