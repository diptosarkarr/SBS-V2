Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our CRM build and management services.

What CRM build options are available? +
Three one-time build tiers: Automation Build at $350, Starter Setup at $750, and Full Build & Training at $3,000.
What's the difference between CRM Build and CRM Management? +
CRM Build is a one-time project to get your CRM set up and configured. CRM Management & Optimization is an ongoing $500/mo service for after your CRM is live — workflow tweaks, data cleanup, monthly review, and ongoing optimization.
Do I need to order both CRM Build and CRM Management? +
No. Many clients start with a one-time build and add ongoing management later, or vice versa if a CRM is already built. Each is priced and ordered separately.
What CRM platform do you build on? +
We also offer Go High Level (GHL) as a dedicated all-in-one CRM platform with its own setup and monthly plans — see our Go High Level page if you specifically want that platform.
What does CRM Management & Optimization include each month? +
Workflow tweaks, data cleanup, a monthly review, and ongoing optimization — keeping your CRM accurate and your automations working as your business changes.
How is CRM Build priced? +
As a one-time fee based on tier: Automation Build at $350, Starter Setup at $750, or Full Build & Training at $3,000.
