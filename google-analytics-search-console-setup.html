Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about analytics and tracking setup.

What's the difference between Google Analytics and Google Search Console? +
Google Analytics (with Tag Manager) tracks visitor traffic, behavior, and conversions on your site. Google Search Console tracks how your site appears and performs in Google Search itself.
Is this included with an SEO plan? +
Yes. Google Analytics & Tag Manager Setup and Google Search Console Setup are included with any SEO plan. Without an SEO plan, both are available à la carte.
How much does GA/GTM setup cost à la carte? +
Google Analytics & Tag Manager (GA/GTM) Setup is $125 one-time when ordered on its own.
Why is GSC Setup a custom quote instead of a fixed price? +
Google Search Console setup and verification can vary depending on your site's current configuration, so it's quoted individually.
Do I need both, or just one? +
Most sites benefit from both — Analytics for on-site behavior, Search Console for search visibility. Either can be set up on its own if you only need one.
