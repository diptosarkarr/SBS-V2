Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our paid advertising management.

Which ad platforms do you manage? +
Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Amazon Ads, and Walmart Ads. Each platform is priced and ordered separately, and you can combine as many as you need.
Do you handle both setup and ongoing management? +
Yes. Google Ads and Meta Ads are priced as a one-time campaign setup plus a separate ongoing monthly management fee. Amazon Ads Management and Walmart Ads Management are priced as monthly tiers that include first month setup and ongoing monthly optimization.
Is there a setup or onboarding fee? +
It depends on the platform. Google Ads and Meta Ads charge a one-time campaign setup fee. Walmart Ads Management adds a $350 one-time onboarding fee on top of your monthly tier, and Amazon Ads Management adds a $500 one-time onboarding fee on top of your monthly tier.
How is pricing structured for each platform? +
Google Ads Ongoing is $500/mo under $2,000 in ad spend, or 20% of spend above that. Amazon Ads Management scales from $500/mo to $7,500/mo based on tier, plus 20% of ad spend. Meta Ads and Walmart Ads are flat monthly management fees.
Can I combine multiple ad platforms? +
Yes. Most clients running ads on more than one platform order each platform's setup and management separately, since each is scoped and priced on its own.
Does this include building my Amazon or Walmart product listings or store? +
No — this page covers ad campaign management only. See our Amazon Listing & Store Setup page for listing and storefront creation.
Is there a minimum ad spend required? +
No fixed minimum, but your ad spend is separate from our management fee and is paid directly to the platform (Google, Meta, Amazon, or Walmart).
