# New images for the November 2026 photo revision

All 39 files are in `images/`. Upload them to the WordPress Media Library **in November 2026 keeping the file names** so they land at `/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/<file name>`, which is the path the pages use. (If they are uploaded in another month, change `2026/11` in the pages.)

- `people-*.webp`: stock photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free for commercial use, no attribution required; photographers listed for the record). Cropped to 4:3, 1200x900.
- `client-*.webp`: real screenshots of live client websites inside a browser frame, 1200x900 (4:3), made with a headless browser on 2026-11-18.
- `site-preview-*.webp`: full-page screenshots (720 px wide, as tall as the site) used by the scroll-on-hover sample cards.

Stock photos must never be described as Sites by Sara staff or clients; the alt texts below describe only what is visible.
