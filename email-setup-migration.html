Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 setup.

Do we have to migrate every email account? +
Not necessarily. We can help decide which accounts need their full history moved and which can start fresh.
What is the difference between an email account and an alias? +
An account is a full mailbox with its own login. An alias is an extra address, like sales@yourcompany.com, that forwards messages into an existing account.
Do you support Microsoft 365 as well as Google Workspace? +
Yes. We set up and migrate email for both.
What if we're a new business setting up email for the first time? +
We can set up your domain, mailboxes, and security records from scratch. Tell us what your team needs in your free consultation.
Will my email stop working during the move? +
We prepare every migration to keep disruption low, and we test mail flow end to end before handoff.
