Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our visibility packages and press release services.

What's included in a Marketing Visibility Package? +
Every tier combines Google Business Profile optimization, keyword tracking, monthly content, and social posting. Higher tiers add multi-platform listings management, Google & Meta ad campaigns, email automation, and PR/media outreach. See the pricing above for exact tier details.
What's the difference between the four tiers? +
Starter, Plus, Growth, and Enterprise scale up in keyword volume, content and social post output, and which additional channels (ads, email automation, PR outreach) are included. We'll recommend the right tier during a free consultation.
Do I need a Visibility Package, or can I order these services individually? +
You can order SEO, Local SEO, Social Media Management, or Google & Meta Ads individually on their own pages. A Visibility Package is for businesses that want all of it coordinated into one strategy and one monthly rate.
Do you offer press release services? +
Yes. We offer standalone Press Release Distribution if you've already written your release, or Press Release Writing & Distribution if you'd like us to write it too — both are one-time services.
Which press release option do I need? +
If you already have a press release written, choose Press Release Distribution. If you need it written from scratch, choose Press Release Writing & Distribution.
Do I need a Visibility Package to order a press release? +
No. Press Release Distribution and Writing & Distribution are standalone one-time services and don't require a Visibility Package.
Which platform is my press release distributed through? +
Your press release is distributed through EIN Presswire or EIN Newswire, putting your announcement in front of media outlets and search engines.
Do higher tiers include paid ad management? +
Yes. The Growth and Enterprise tiers include Google and Meta ad campaign management as part of the package. Lower tiers focus on organic channels.
