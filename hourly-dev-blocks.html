Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our hourly and block-based development services.

What hourly services are available? +
Five hourly services: Emergency Site Repair ($195/hr), Website Backup ($149/hr), Website Migration ($225/hr), Website Updates - Rush ($195/hr), and Block of Hours (minimum 8-hour block, saves $10/hr).
What's the difference between hourly billing and Block of Hours? +
Standard hourly billing charges the per-service rate as work happens. Block of Hours requires a minimum 8-hour commitment and saves $10/hr off the standard rate.
What's included in Website Updates - Rush? +
Website Updates - Rush is $195/hr for urgent hourly work, includes a 30% rush fee, and is billed by the quarter hour.
What is WHAM Plus Hour Blocks? +
A monthly retainer for ongoing development hours, billed at the rate of 1 hour of web design/development work per hour used. Tier 1 gives 3 hrs/mo for $150/mo, Tier 2 gives 6 hrs/mo for $300/mo, and Tier 3 gives 9 hrs/mo for $450/mo.
Can I pay annually for WHAM Plus Hour Blocks? +
Yes. Tier 1 is $1,800/yr, Tier 2 is $3,600/yr, and Tier 3 is $5,400/yr — the same monthly rate billed as one annual payment.
Is there a minimum commitment for WHAM Plus Hour Blocks? +
Yes, a 12-month minimum is required for WHAM Plus Hour Blocks.
