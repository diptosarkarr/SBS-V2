Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our local SEO and GBP services.

What's included in the Local Domination Package? +
Keyword assessment and research, backlinking, citations, bookmarking, and social backlinking, and ongoing Google Business Profile / Maps management, for $750/mo.
What's the difference between GBP Setup and 3-Pack Optimization? +
Google Business Profile Setup ($900 one-time) creates and verifies a new profile from scratch. Google 3-Pack Optimization ($650 or $275 one-time) is geo-targeted ranking optimization for an existing profile within a set radius.
Do you offer a smaller radius option for 3-Pack Optimization? +
Yes. Google 3-Pack Optimization is available for a 5 square mile radius ($650 one-time) or a 10 square mile radius ($275 one-time).
What if I have multiple business locations? +
The Additional Location Add-On ($250/mo) extends Google Business Profile updates and local optimization to an additional location.
How does Reputation Management work? +
Reputation Management ($149/mo) covers review monitoring and reputation monitoring and response, so negative reviews get a prompt, professional reply while more positive reviews get encouraged.
Is the Local Domination Package a one-time or ongoing service? +
Ongoing. Local Domination Package is a monthly retainer at $750/mo.
What's the difference between the Local Domination Package and a Marketing Visibility Package? +
Local Domination Package ($750/mo) is focused on local SEO and Google Business Profile management. Marketing Visibility Package ($1,500-$7,500/mo) bundles GBP optimization with 12-15 keywords, 2 content pieces per month, and 6 social posts per month, for businesses that want local SEO, content, and social handled together.
What's the difference between Local SEO Plans and the Local Domination Package? +
They're two ways to structure the same kind of ongoing local SEO work. Local Domination Package is a single flat-rate retainer at $750/mo. Local SEO Plans offer 4 graduated monthly tiers (Starter $350/mo, Growth $500/mo, Bloom $650/mo, Harvest $2,250/mo) built around weekly Google Business Profile updates and ongoing local optimization, for businesses that want to start smaller or scale further than the flat-rate package.
What are the Marketing Visibility Package tiers? +
Four tiers: Basic at $1,500/mo, Standard at $2,750/mo, Premium at $5,500/mo, and Elite at $7,500/mo.
