Client FAQs

Client Support Portal FAQs

Answers to common questions about using our client portal, ticket turnaround times, and maintenance coverage.

How quickly will my support portal ticket be addressed? +
Support requests are reviewed promptly during our active operating hours: Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM MT, and Saturday through Sunday from 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM MT. Clients on active WHAM maintenance plans receive priority ticket queuing.
What information should I include in my support ticket? +
To help us diagnose and fix issues quickly, please provide your website URL, a clear description of the issue or update required, exact error messages or screenshots, and mention any recent plugins, themes, or credentials updated.
Is technical support included in my maintenance plan? +
Yes. If your site is covered under one of our WordPress Hosting and Maintenance (WHAM) packages, routine updates, core security patches, backups, and routine maintenance assistance are included. Larger custom coding or site overhauls can be handled via our Hourly Dev Blocks or project retainers.
What if my website is completely offline or hacked? +
If your site is experiencing critical downtime or a severe security compromise, please submit this form immediately and select Urgent in the urgency field. High-priority tickets automatically alert on-duty engineers for immediate triage.
Can I request new features or design changes through this portal? +
Yes. You can use this portal form to submit requests for new pages, layout adjustments, ecommerce updates, or plugin integrations. We will evaluate the scope and provide a quick estimate or confirm execution.
