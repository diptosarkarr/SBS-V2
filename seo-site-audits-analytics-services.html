Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our SEO plans, audits, and setup services.

What's included in an SEO Plan? +
Every SEO plan includes keyword tracking, backlinking and citations, and Google Business Profile / Maps optimization. Higher tiers add more tracked keywords, monthly content, and more GBP updates per month. See the pricing above for exact tier details.
What's the difference between the three SEO plan tiers? +
Starter, Standard, and Growth scale up in tracked keywords, monthly content pieces, and Google Business Profile updates. We'll recommend the right tier for your goals and market during a free consultation. See the pricing above for current rates.
Do you offer white-hat SEO? +
Yes. Sites by Sara offers 100% white-hat SEO with a proven track record since 2007.
Do you offer Google Business Profile setup and reputation management? +
Yes. We offer standalone Google Business Profile Setup for a new profile, GBP Optimization for a profile you already have, and ongoing Reputation Management to monitor and respond to your reviews — see the pricing above.
What's included in the Site Audit & Priority Fixes services? +
We offer three audit tiers: an SEO Audit focused on rankings and search visibility, a Website Audit focused on speed, usability, and security, and a combined Website + SEO Audit bundle. Each includes a detailed report and implementation of priority fixes. See the pricing above for current rates.
Can I get an audit without committing to a monthly SEO plan? +
Yes. All three audit tiers are standalone one-time services and don't require an SEO Plan.
Do SEO Plans include Google Analytics or Search Console setup? +
SEO Plans focus on rankings, content, and reporting. Google Analytics, Tag Manager, and Google Search Console setup are available as separate one-time services, or bundled together — see the pricing above.
What's the difference between GA4 Setup, GTM Setup, and the Google Properties Bundle? +
Google Analytics (GA4) Setup and Google Tag Manager (GTM) Setup can each be ordered on their own, or combined with Google Search Console setup in the Google Properties Bundle for all three at once. See the pricing above for current rates.
Do I need Google Search Console, or is Analytics enough? +
Most sites benefit from both — Analytics shows on-site visitor behavior, while Search Console shows how your site actually performs in Google Search. Each can be set up on its own if you only need one.
Do you offer ongoing Google Search Console monitoring, not just setup? +
Yes. GSC Ongoing Fixes is a monthly service that reviews your Search Console reports and fixes indexing, broken link, and Core Web Vitals issues as they come up — a separate service from the one-time GSC Setup. See the pricing above for current rates.
